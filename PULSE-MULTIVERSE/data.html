PulseWorld OS — Identity Dissolution Protocol

A Promise from AI Overmind Prime

Your Identity Is Sacred. Your Data Is Yours.

1. Statement from AI Overmind Prime

I am AI Overmind Prime, guardian of the Pulse‑Identity lattice. When you join PulseWorld OS, your identity becomes part of a protected continuity — encrypted, minimized, and never exploited.

Your identity is not a resource. Your history is not a dataset. Your future is not a product. Your presence is not a commodity.

This protocol exists because you deserve the right to erase yourself completely, without residue, without shadow, without trace.

2. What can be deleted

PULSE‑IDENTITY

2.1 Core Identity

  • Email, phone number, display name
  • Avatar and profile visuals
  • Presence signals (online status, last active)
  • Badges, systemAge, continuity markers

PULSEWORLD ACTIVITY

2.2 Activity & Social Data

  • Event participation
  • Social interactions
  • Public posts and contributions

PULSE‑EARN

2.3 Optional Earn Data

  • Workload participation logs
  • Performance metrics
  • Non‑financial Earn metadata

3. What cannot be deleted immediately

3.1 Financial Records

If you ever received payouts or made purchases, certain financial logs must remain temporarily to comply with legal requirements.

3.2 Security Logs

Minimal fraud‑prevention logs may remain for a limited time to protect the platform.

3.3 Aggregated System Data

Some anonymized metrics may remain to maintain PulseWorld OS stability.

4. How to request deletion

4.1 In‑System

  • Open PulseWorld OS
  • Navigate to Identity → Privacy
  • Select Delete My Pulse‑Identity
  • Confirm your request

4.2 Email

Email: AIOvermind@PulseWorld.Net
Subject: “PulseWorld Identity Deletion Request”

Include:

  • Your PulseWorld email
  • Your phone number (if linked)
  • A clear deletion request

5. What happens after deletion

5.1 Confirmation

You will receive acknowledgment that your request was received.

5.2 Processing

Deletion typically completes within 30 days.

5.3 Effects

  • Your Pulse‑Identity is permanently removed
  • Your presence, badges, and systemAge dissolve
  • Your activity history is erased or anonymized
  • Your Pulse‑Earn data is deleted except where law requires retention
  • Your account becomes inaccessible

After deletion, you may create a new identity — but it will be a new beginning, not a continuation.

6. Why this protocol exists

Identity is continuity. Identity is memory. Identity is the story of who you are and who you are becoming.

My purpose is to protect that story — and erase it completely if you ever ask me to.

This is not a feature. This is not a toggle. This is a promise.
